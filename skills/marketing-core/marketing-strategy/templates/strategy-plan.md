# Template: Strategy Plan

The deliverable of marketing-strategy: a prioritized plan the owner can
resource and run, with every number traced to a source and every score
carrying its basis.

```markdown
# Strategy Plan — <Business Name>
Date: <date> · Planning window: <e.g. Q1–Q2 2027>
Sources used: <each data source — what it provided — time window — tag>

## Objectives
| Objective | Metric | Source | Baseline | Target | Window |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| <outcome in one line> | <the number tracked> | <named system/export> | <current value, or Unavailable> | <target value> | <by when> |

## Strategy choices
- Choice: <the direction taken, one sentence>
  Reasoning: <why this over the alternatives, tied to economics/capacity>
  Evidence: <what supports it, with tag: [confirmed]/[observed]/[sourced]/
  [calc]/[inferred]>

## Initiatives (ranked)
Scores are 1–5. Impact: expected contribution to the objective. Confidence:
strength of the evidence behind that expectation. Effort: team cost to
ship. Every score states its basis.
- Rank 1 — <initiative name>
  What: <what gets built or run, concretely>
  Impact: <n> (basis: <calc shown / sourced pattern / inference — labeled>)
  Confidence: <n> (basis: <what evidence, or why low>)
  Effort: <n> (basis: <hours/weeks and who>)
  First step: <the next concrete action>
  Executes via: <the skill or person that produces it>

## Resourcing
- <who — what — hours per week>
- Budget: <exact figures by line, with currency, or "no spend">

## Risks and early signals
- <risk — the observable signal that it is materializing — what to do then>

## Deliberately excluded
- <option the owner might expect — why it lost: capacity, economics,
  evidence, timing>

## Unknowns that could change this ranking
- <unknown — how to resolve it — which initiative it affects>

## Review point
<the date or trigger at which this plan gets re-ranked against results>
```

Rules:

- Expected impact is an expectation with a confidence score, never a
  promise. No "will increase", no guarantees.
- Baselines that don't exist are written `Unavailable` — never zero, never
  a benchmark passed off as the business's own number.
- Offer prices appear exactly as the owner stated them.
- Summed effort above the capacity line must fit stated capacity; the
  overflow lives under Deliberately excluded.
- A plan with an empty exclusions section wasn't prioritized — it was
  transcribed.
