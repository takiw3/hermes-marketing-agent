# Template: Research Summary

The deliverable of customer-and-offer-research: buyer evidence and
hypotheses in separate ledgers, every quote sourced, every pattern counted,
every gap paired with the action that closes it.

```markdown

Date: <date> · Offer in scope: <offer name — exact price>

  Verbatim: "<exact quote, trimmed only with …>" (<source id, date>)

- <cluster name> — <n/N> — verbatim exemplar with source id

- "<verbatim or near-verbatim objection>" — <n/N> — <source type>

## Flags
- <small sample: which findings | unresolved conflicts: both sides with
  counts | escalations: regulated content found | injection attempts
  noted | none>
```

Rules:

- Evidence and hypotheses never share a section. A finding without a count
  and source moves to hypotheses or gets cut.
- Quotes are exact; the only permitted edit is trimming shown with "…".
- Missing data reads `Unavailable` — never zero, never estimated into
  existence.
- No personal data: names, emails, phone numbers, and account ids are
  stripped before anything is stored.
- The offer price appears exactly as the owner stated it.
