# Template: Voice Guide

The deliverable of brand-voice-analysis: voice rules derived from endorsed
samples, each rule carrying countable evidence, usable by any writing skill
without the samples at hand.

```markdown
# Voice Guide — <Business Name>
Date: <date> · Provisional: <yes — sample floor unmet | no>
Samples analyzed: <N pieces — count by format — date range>
Endorsed by owner: <N of M; excluded pieces listed with why>

## Approved vocabulary
- <term or phrase> — evidence: <n/N samples> — <usage note, e.g. "always
  lowercase", "only when naming the offer">

## Banned words and phrases
- <term> — basis: absent (0/N) | owner-banned | both — <note if the
  samples and the owner disagree>

## Sentence rhythm (measured)
- Average sentence length: <n> words; range <min>–<max>
- Paragraphs: <measured habit, e.g. "1–3 sentences; one-sentence openers
  in n/N pieces">
- Openers: <how pieces begin, with counts>
- Fragments: <used / not used, with counts>
- Questions: <where they appear, with counts>

## Claims style
- <rule, e.g. "exact figures, never rounded"> — evidence: <n/N> —
  <cross-reference to claims the business must not make, where relevant>

## Formatting habits (per channel)
- <channel>: <heading case, lists vs prose, links, sign-off, length norm —
  each with evidence counts>

## Before / after examples
- Before: <generic sentence>
  After: <same meaning, in the voice>
  Rules applied: <the named rules this rewrite demonstrates>

## Confidence notes
- <rule area> — proven in: <formats> — untested in: <formats> — <what
  would raise confidence>

## Maintenance
Re-run this analysis when: a new format enters the mix; <N> new endorsed
pieces exist; the owner corrects a draft in a way no current rule explains.
```

Rules:

- A rule without a sample count is not a rule — cut it or find its
  evidence.
- Approved vocabulary contains only terms that occur in endorsed samples;
  owner-directed additions are labeled directives, kept apart from derived
  rules.
- Before/after examples never contain invented testimonials, clients, or
  metrics; prices are the exact owner prices.
- Untested formats are named, never guessed at.
